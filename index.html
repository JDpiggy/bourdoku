How to play 👑

  • Place one king in every row, column, and colored region.
  • No two kings may touch, even diagonally.
  • Kings on the same diagonal are fine if more than one square apart.
  • Tap a square to cycle: empty → X → king → empty.
  • Right-click to toggle an X note.
  • X marks are just your notes — they never count as kings.
  • Undo / Reset anytime. Conflicting kings glow red.

Tip: Work out exclusions yourself — auto-elimination is off.

Debug: Ctrl+Shift+D shows live state overlay.
